Leadership

The Office is headed by a Director appointed by the Governor on the proposal of the Minister of Economy. The Director signs every official release.

Gulbarchyn Ryskulova

Gulbarchyn Ryskulova

Director · since 2017

Born in Talas in 1966. A statistician with the Office since 1993, she worked on the price index and the household survey before leading the 2018 census as deputy director. Director since 2017; she signed the census tables of 2019 and Directive 2026/24's census of business entities into the field.

Deputies and officials

Deputy Director, economic statistics

National accounts, prices, wages, the business census

Post vacant since September 2026; the Director acts.

Deputy Director, population and social statistics

Census, population registers, health, education, tourism

Structure

Section of national accounts and prices

GDP estimate, consumer price index (about 400 goods and services in the three cities and four district seats), producer prices.

Tel. +996-39-2-26-40-14

Section of labour and incomes

The quarterly bulletin Wages and Incomes, from the payroll reports of employers with five or more staff.

Tel. +996-39-2-26-40-17

Section of population censuses

The 2018 census tables and volumes; preparation of the next population census, provisionally 2028.

Tel. +996-39-2-26-40-21

Business census office

Runs the census of business entities to 30 November 2026: enumerators, the census slip and the TSO-26 questionnaire.

Tel. +996-39-2-26-40-26

Reading room and dissemination

Publications, tables on request and copies at cost. Monday to Friday 9:00–17:00, by appointment.

Tel. +996-39-2-26-40-30
