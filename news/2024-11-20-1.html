Health statistics, 2023: life expectancy 71.4 years

Life expectancy at birth in 2023 was 71.4 years (men 67.2, women 75.6). Infant mortality was 14 per 1,000 live births. The leading causes of death were diseases of the circulatory system, cancers and injuries. See indicators.
