18 June 2019

Census of 2018: main results published

Population Census of 2018: Main Results. Statistical tables is signed for printing today. The official population of the whole territory is 4,626,000, of which the enumerated Zone 2,630,000 and the Autonomous Tribal Regions an estimate of 2,000,000.

The tables are in the reading room and on this site: results of the 2018 census.

G. Ryskulova, Director
