20 July 2026 · TSO 2026/09

Census of business entities: the questionnaire TSO-26 approved

By order of the Director the questionnaire TSO-26 (four pages, in the three languages) and the census slip are approved. The slip is proof of enumeration and is asked for by the Register of Firms until 30 November 2026.

G. Ryskulova, Director
