30 June 2025

Gross domestic product, 2024: US$18.4 billion

The official estimate of gross domestic product for 2024 is US$18.4 billion at current prices, with real growth of 1.5%. The estimate covers the whole territory and includes an imputed estimate for the Autonomous Tribal Regions.
