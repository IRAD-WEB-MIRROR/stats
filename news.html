Press service news

Releases of the Territorial Statistics Office, newest first. Figures are published at 10:00 Irad Time on the day shown.

2026

  • 15 October 2026
    Census of business entities begins

    The first census of business entities in the Zone runs from 15 October to 30 November 2026. Please receive the enumerators. See the census.

  • 12 October 2026
    Consumer prices, September 2026

    Consumer prices rose 7.9% on September 2025. Domestic food prices were stable or lower after the harvest. See prices.

  • 1 September 2026 · TSO 2026/14
    Enumerators for the census of business entities: training complete

    Training of 1,140 enumerators and 96 instructors for the census of business entities ended on 29 August at Talas State University, the Nurzhan Polytechnic and School No. 3, Iyık Janyl.

  • 14 August 2026
    Wages and Incomes, second quarter 2026

    The quarterly bulletin is out. Gross wages are reported in som, as paid. See wages and pensions.

  • 20 July 2026 · TSO 2026/09
    Census of business entities: the questionnaire TSO-26 approved

    By order of the Director the questionnaire TSO-26 (four pages, in the three languages) and the census slip are approved. The slip is proof of enumeration and is asked for by the Register of Firms until 30 November 2026.

  • 12 May 2026
    Consumer prices, April 2026

    Prices rose 5.6% on April 2025. Imported goods priced from the dollar rose faster than domestic goods; fuel and medicines led the rise.

  • 16 March 2026
    Wages and Incomes, 2025: average wage 3,250 som

    The average gross monthly wage in 2025 was 3,250 som: Nurzhan 3,900, Talas 3,550, Iyık Janyl 2,450, the six districts 2,150. The highest was in oil and gas, about 9,800 som; the lowest in farming, about 1,600.

  • 14 January 2026
    Consumer prices, 2025: 4.8%

    Consumer prices rose 4.8% in 2025, December on December (2024: 5.1%). Food rose 3.9%, other goods 5.7%, services 5.2%.

2025

  • 30 June 2025
    Gross domestic product, 2024: US$18.4 billion

    The official estimate of gross domestic product for 2024 is US$18.4 billion at current prices, with real growth of 1.5%. The estimate covers the whole territory and includes an imputed estimate for the Autonomous Tribal Regions.

  • 15 January 2025
    Consumer prices, 2024: 5.1%

    Consumer prices rose 5.1% in 2024, December on December. Bread and flour rose 2.2% after the good wheat harvest; heating and electricity were unchanged.

2024

  • 20 November 2024
    Health statistics, 2023: life expectancy 71.4 years

    Life expectancy at birth in 2023 was 71.4 years (men 67.2, women 75.6). Infant mortality was 14 per 1,000 live births. The leading causes of death were diseases of the circulatory system, cancers and injuries. See indicators.

  • 25 April 2024
    Tourism, 2023: 214,000 foreign arrivals

    Border records show about 214,000 arrivals of foreign citizens in 2023, a third of them for leisure, pilgrimage or family visits. Arrivals peaked in August, the month of the Feast of St. Janyl.

2023

  • 30 August 2023
    Drought and the harvest: grain estimate

    On reports from the district administrations, the 2023 wheat harvest in the Zone is estimated 14% below 2022, with the largest falls on rain-fed land in Chu and Kara-Buura districts.

2020

  • 28 September 2020
    Census of 2018: volume on the labour force

    The last of the detailed volumes, on the labour force, employment and education, is published. With the volumes on housing (2019) and migration (2020) it completes the census programme.

  • 30 March 2020
    Census of 2018: volume on migration

    The volume gives place of birth, previous residence and year of arrival for the enumerated population of the Zone. Workers abroad for more than a year were not counted in the resident population.
