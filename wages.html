Subsistence minimum

The subsistence minimum is the monthly cost of a minimum set of food, goods and services for one person. The Office calculates it every quarter from its price survey; food makes up about 55% of it.

Employment

About 690,000 formal jobs in the Zone, of which about 260,000 (38%) in the public sector and state enterprises. The minimum wage has been 1,000 som a month since 1 January 2024 (800 som before).
